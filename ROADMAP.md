# ChemE Studio — development roadmap

We work **one chapter at a time** and finish it before starting the next.

## Workflow for every chapter (stage gate)
1. **Audit** the chapter against the syllabus and the textbooks: list every sub-topic and what is missing.
2. **Mechanism first.** Each sub-topic opens with a 3-D or animated scene that shows *how the equipment or process works*. A step-by-step walkthrough explains each part, labels sit at the edges with leader lines, and controls change the scene.
3. **Material balance.** Each chapter gets its own balance (stage, overall, component) as an interactive flow diagram whose numbers update live.
4. **Design calculation.** Correlations, numbers and charts come second, inside a "Design calculation" panel.
5. **Check yourself.** Cause → effect list, key equations and a quiz.
6. **Accuracy and sources.** Standard correlations within their valid range, numbers checked against textbook values, and a **Sources** list on every topic (textbook + original paper for each correlation).
7. **QA.** No console errors; screenshots in light and dark mode and at narrow width; no text overlapping lines or other text.

## Shared engine improvements (app/core.js)
- 3-D labels are callouts at the edge with leader lines and no overlap; a toggle hides them.
- Plot legends move automatically to the emptiest corner, and plot text gets a halo.
- Narrow 3-D views: labels are cut to half the view width with an ellipsis, so the two label columns never collide.
- `UI.walk()` step-by-step mechanism stepper; `UI.more()` collapsible design panel.
- Lab pages: `bench.view(s, V, rows, S)` receives the sim, `bench.walk` adds a procedure walkthrough, `lab.refs` shows a Sources list in the Theory tab.
- Prev/next topic buttons wrap on narrow screens (long titles no longer cause sideways scrolling).
